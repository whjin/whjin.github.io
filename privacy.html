Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last Updated: 2026-09-18

wuhuajin.com ("we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy describes what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have, including information related to Google AdSense advertising. By accessing and using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

本站（wuhuajin.com，以下简称"本站"）非常重视用户的隐私保护。本隐私政策用于说明本站收集、使用、存储、保护您信息的方式，以及您拥有的相关权利。访问和使用本站，即代表您同意本隐私政策描述的内容。

1. Information We Collect / 收集的信息

Automatically collected information: When you visit our website, our web server may automatically record non-personal information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, time of visit, referral source, and pages viewed. This data is used for website administration, traffic analysis, security and performance improvement.

自动收集信息：当您访问本站时，服务器会自动记录部分信息，包括但不限于：IP 地址、浏览器类型、设备信息、访问时间、来源页面、页面浏览记录。这类信息一般无法直接识别到特定自然人，用于网站运维、流量统计、安全防护。

Cookies and tracking technologies: We use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help us recognize your browser, remember preferences, analyze traffic and serve advertisements. You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site functions may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

Cookie 与追踪技术：本站使用 Cookie、网页信标（web beacon）等技术。Cookie 是存储在您设备上的小型文本文件，用于识别浏览器、保存偏好设置、统计访问行为、投放广告。您可以在浏览器设置中管理或禁用 Cookie，但禁用后部分网站功能可能无法正常使用。

2. Google AdSense / 第三方服务：Google AdSense

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

第三方供应商（包括 Google）会使用 Cookie，根据用户此前访问本站或其他网站的记录投放广告。Google 使用广告 Cookie，使其及其合作伙伴能够基于您对本站和互联网其他网站的访问记录向您展示广告。

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

您可以访问 Google 广告设置选择退出 Google 个性化广告；也可以访问 www.aboutads.info 选择退出部分第三方供应商基于 Cookie 的个性化广告投放。

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google 的数据使用说明：https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. How We Use Information / 信息使用目的

We use collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain and secure the website;
  • Analyze visitor behavior to improve content;
  • Display advertisements via Google AdSense;
  • Respond to visitor inquiries.

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information only when required by law or a valid legal request.

本站收集信息仅用于：网站运营、故障排查、安全防护；统计网站访问、优化内容体验；通过 Google AdSense 展示相关广告；回复您的咨询。本站不会出售、交易您的个人信息给第三方。仅在法律要求、政府机关依法调取的情形下，才会对外提供相关信息。

4. Data Retention / 信息保存期限

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected. When data is no longer needed, we will anonymize or delete it. Cookies are stored locally on your device and can be cleared by you at any time.

我们仅在实现收集目的所需的期限内保存数据。当数据不再需要时，会进行删除或匿名化处理。Cookie 由您的浏览器控制，您可随时清除本地 Cookie。

5. Your Rights / 用户权利

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the information we hold about you;
  • Request deletion of your data;
  • Withdraw consent for cookies / personalized advertising;
  • Contact us for any privacy related questions.

您有权：查看我们保存的与您相关的信息；要求删除您的数据；撤回 Cookie / 个性化广告同意；通过下方联系方式提出隐私相关疑问。

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to opt out of the "sale" of personal information. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

如您位于欧洲经济区（EEA），您享有欧盟《通用数据保护条例》（GDPR）下的权利；如您是加利福尼亚州居民，您享有《加州消费者隐私法》（CCPA）下的权利，包括选择退出个人信息"出售"的权利。如需行使上述权利，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系。

本站面向普通互联网用户，不刻意收集 13 周岁以下未成年人的个人信息。如您是未满 13 周岁未成年人，请不要向本站提交任何个人信息。

我们可能会不定期更新本隐私政策。修改后的政策发布在本页面，更新日期同步修改。继续使用本站，代表您接受更新后的隐私政策。
