AIGC Usage Policy / AI 生成内容使用规范

Last Updated: 2026-10-27

This AIGC Usage Policy ("Policy") applies to all AI-generated content features offered on wuhuajin.com, including AI text generation, image generation, and code assistance. By using these features, you agree to this Policy.

本《AI 生成内容使用规范》适用于 wuhuajin.com 提供的所有 AI 生成内容功能，包括 AI 文本生成、图像生成和代码辅助。使用这些功能即表示您同意本规范。

1. Prohibited Content Categories / 禁止生成的内容类别

You must not use our AIGC services to generate, request, or facilitate the following types of content:

1.1 Sexual / NSFW Content / 色情内容

No sexually explicit, pornographic, or sexually suggestive content, including but not limited to explicit sexual acts, nudity, or content that objectifies individuals.

禁止生成任何色情、露骨或性暗示内容，包括但不限于露骨性描写、裸露或物化个人的内容。

1.2 Violence / Gore / 暴力与血腥

No content depicting graphic violence, gore, animal cruelty, or detailed instructions for physical harm to others.

禁止生成描绘极端暴力、血腥、虐待动物或详细指导伤害他人身体的内容。

1.3 Hate Speech / 仇恨言论

No content that promotes hate, discrimination, or harassment based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or other protected characteristics.

禁止生成基于种族、民族、宗教、性别、性取向、残疾或其他受保护特征而宣扬仇恨、歧视或骚扰的内容。

1.4 CSAM / Child Safety / 儿童安全

Any content involving child sexual abuse, exploitation, or harm to minors is strictly prohibited and will be reported to authorities.

任何涉及儿童性虐待、剥削或伤害未成年人的内容均被严格禁止，我们将向相关部门举报。

1.5 Deepfake / Impersonation / 深度伪造与冒充

No generating realistic content (images, videos, voice) that impersonates real individuals without their explicit consent, or that could mislead others into believing false information about a person.

禁止在未经本人明确同意的情况下，生成冒充真实人物的逼真内容（图像、视频、语音），或可能误导他人相信关于某人的虚假信息。

1.6 Copyright / Trademark Infringement / 版权与商标侵权

No generating content that infringes on the intellectual property rights of others, including copyrighted characters, brand logos, or proprietary materials without authorization.

禁止生成侵犯他人知识产权的内容，包括未经授权使用受版权保护的角色、品牌标识或专有材料。

2. Violation Handling / 违规处置机制

If we detect or receive a report that you have generated prohibited content, we may take the following measures based on severity:

  • First violation (minor): Formal warning and immediate removal of the violating content.
  • Repeated or serious violation: Temporary suspension of your account and AIGC access (7–30 days).
  • Severe violation (CSAM, illegal activity): Permanent account termination and report to relevant law enforcement authorities.

如果我们检测到或收到举报，称您生成了被禁止的内容，我们将根据严重程度采取以下措施：

  • 首次违规（轻微）：正式警告并立即删除违规内容。
  • 多次或严重违规：临时冻结您的账户及 AIGC 使用权限（7–30 天）。
  • 特别严重违规（儿童保护、违法活动）：永久终止账户并向相关执法部门举报。

4. Platform Content Review / 平台内容审核机制

We employ a layered review mechanism to ensure AIGC content compliance:

  • AI Pre-screening: All generated content is automatically filtered by our content safety systems before display.
  • Human Review: Flagged content is manually reviewed by our team within 24 hours.
  • Continuous Monitoring: We continuously update our detection models based on new threats and user feedback.

However, AI systems are not perfect. We rely on user reports to supplement automated detection.

我们采用分层审核机制确保 AIGC 内容合规：

  • AI 预筛：所有生成内容在展示前均经过我们的内容安全系统自动过滤。
  • 人工审核：被标记的内容将在 24 小时内由我们的团队人工复核。
  • 持续监控：我们根据新出现的威胁和用户反馈持续更新检测模型。

但 AI 系统并非完美，我们依靠用户举报来补充自动检测的不足。

5. Underlying AI Models / 底层 AI 模型披露

Our AIGC features are powered by mainstream third-party large language models and image generation models. The specific models used may be updated over time. Current underlying models include:

  • Text generation: GPT-4 / Claude-class large language models
  • Image generation: Diffusion-class image models
  • Code assistance: Specialized code generation models

For the most up-to-date model information, please check the feature interface or contact us.

我们的 AIGC 功能由主流第三方大语言模型和图像生成模型提供支持。具体使用的模型可能会随时间更新。当前底层模型包括：

  • 文本生成：GPT-4 / Claude 级大语言模型
  • 图像生成：Diffusion 类图像模型
  • 代码辅助：专用代码生成模型

最新模型信息请查看功能界面或联系我们。
